SwapCityClassic

      
A top-down driving game on the real streets of Minneapolis, MN — every road, block and building is the real place.

      
This is built for a keyboard and mouse. On a phone or tablet there is no way to drive yet.

      

        Arrows / WASDdrive
        Spacehandbrake
        Eget out, get in, swap into a car
        Mtake or drop a mission
        GGoogle Earth view
        Tsettings
      

      
Click or press any key to start
